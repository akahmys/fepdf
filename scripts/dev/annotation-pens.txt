# The pens of ROADMAP AA-4g, drawn with the pointer and keys a reader uses.
#
#   ./target/debug/fepdf-gui --capture scripts/dev/annotation-pens.txt --shots /tmp/pens
#
# A shot after choosing a pen lets the page finish drawing: a click before then has no
# page under it. The bottom of the window is left alone: the view controls come up there
# when the pointer reaches it, over the page, and take the click (`view_controls.rs`).

open samples/constitution.pdf

# A polygon's corners clicked, and Enter.
pen polygon
shot 01-polygon-pen
clickat 700 60
clickat 830 60
clickat 765 125
key Enter

# A polyline's points clicked briskly and the last double-clicked, which egui counts as a
# triple-click (2026-10-11).
pen polyline
shot 02-polyline-pen
clickat 700 700
clickat 820 740
dblclickat 940 690

# Boxes: a watermark that fills its box, a colour bar, a redaction mark in black.
pen watermark 下書き
drag 330 470 820 560
pen printer_mark
clicklabel カラーバー
drag 600 20 800 45
pen redact 削除済み
clicklabel 黒
drag 500 220 700 240

# A caret where the click was, a projection's comment, and a popup for the polygon.
pen caret 追加

pen projection 計測値の確認

pen popup
